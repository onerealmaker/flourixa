About Flourixa Tech

A Canadian technology company focused on wellbeing.

Flourixa Technologies Inc. develops and operates digital platforms across wellbeing, education, and financial empowerment. The company is headquartered in Calgary, Alberta.

Our mission

To help people and communities flourish.

The name Flourixa comes from the idea of flourishing: living well across every part of life. Wellbeing sits at the centre of that idea, and it is the focus of the company's flagship product, FriendJo.

A flourishing life also depends on the opportunity to learn and grow, and on the stability to plan ahead with confidence. Flourixa's portfolio reflects this, with products in education and financial empowerment alongside its work in wellbeing.

Every Flourixa product is held to the same standards: careful handling of personal data, practical value for its users, and long-term operation and support.

Areas of focus

Three areas. One purpose.

01 · Wellbeing

Healthier workplaces

Helping organizations recognize strain early and support their people, while protecting individual privacy. FriendJo

02 · Education

03 · Financial empowerment

Stronger businesses

Confident French, a few minutes a day.

This website does not collect personal information. It has no forms or user accounts, and it does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

To display its typeface, the website loads fonts from Google Fonts. When it does, your browser shares your IP address with Google, as with any web request. Google's handling of that information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
